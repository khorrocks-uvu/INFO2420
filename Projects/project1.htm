Web Site Critiques

Jami Lin

https://www.jamilin.com
  1. My first impression of the web site is that it has less modern feel, but is also very colorful and attention grabbing without being overwhelming.
  2. The site organizes all the most important and immediate information in the graphic header, which links to specific services right below. When you scroll down, specific services are expanded upon in clean columns without the user having to click on any links.
  3. The site structure makes it easy to find information. Users seeking a specific service do not have to put in much effort to find what they need. The site presents its services very clearly.
  4. The graphics and visual design of the web site both helps and potentially harms the web site. The various graphics make each service stand out and help the web site be unforgettable in style. At the same time, the less modern design could set some potential clients off. Some users may see the web site and business as less legitimate due to elements being more simplistic and static. Additionally, the pop up graphic may annoy users or give the web site an even less legitimate feeling.
  5. The home page loaded very quickly, which naturally follows from the more simple elements used and the graphics not being excessive in quality.
  6. The text was very readable. The text on the header graphic was large with a simple font, and was surrounded with a gentle white outline that provided extra readability when the text overlapped with a graphic element. The additional text on the page continued to be very readable, with the background color being white with black text that was formatted neatly with good spacing, and even made use of emphasized and underlined text so important details could jump out without being jarring. While the plain background for the main body might appear plain, it compliments well with the top portion of the page, which has plenty of color.
  7. The navigation was well done. The top bar navigation allowed all services to be shown clearly, enabling clients to quickly navigate to the service they are interested in rather than tabbing through various menus. Additionally, primary services were listed and once again linked to in the main body where they are further described. The design gives users multiple avenues for finding information on the services they want and prevents the site from feeling bloated.
  8. The design scheme of the web site was not repeated across pages. In fact, the linked pages for various services takes users to entirely new web sites with completely different design schemes. This can be jarring for a user, especially one who may want to look through each provided service, and makes it so the linked pages vary in quality and complexity.
  9. The web site varies the size and positioning of elements when sizing down, thus allowing the site to be accessible in multiple contexts. While the elements look less polished, such as the navigation appearing a bit too tall and the text in the main body seeming too stretched out, it is a minor issue and does not cause a difference in readability or functionality.
  10. The web site having information common in the footer of a page right below the header graphic and navigation was a unique element, as most web sites I visit shove contact information and social platforms as out of the way as possible. In this case, it goes well with the design as the web site, furthering the quality of providing all important information and navigation links at the very top of the page while still allowing users to scroll down if they're curious about the additional information provided on certain services. The site is thus very straightforward and does not demand much out of the user, instead giving the impression that the business wants to make navigation and information-seeking as easy as possible for the user.
